## Limitations

- RAGAS scores come from a **single judging run**; variance across repeated
  judgements was not quantified.
- Models are named by alias (`gpt-4o-mini`, `gpt-4o`) rather than pinned to dated
  snapshots, so a later reproduction may resolve to a different model version.
- Faithfulness is reported for RAG only. The proposal (§8.2) anticipated it for
  both systems, but the metric measures whether an answer is grounded in retrieved
  context, and the baseline has no retrieved context — it is undefined there.
- Exact Match is a conservative metric for this task and understates the system;
  the gap between EM (0.200) and F1 (0.564) quantifies that effect.
